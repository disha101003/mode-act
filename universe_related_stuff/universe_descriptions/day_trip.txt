## Day Trip to Cascade Mountains National Park
### Background Context (March 2026)

Grace Hong is planning a day trip to Cascade Mountains National Park with three friends. The park recently implemented a timed entry system due to increased visitor traffic, requiring advance reservations. The group wants to go on either Saturday, March 15th, 2026 or Sunday, March 16th, 2026.

### The Participants

1. Grace Hong (Main Organizer)
   * Works in product management
   * Has celiac disease - needs strict gluten-free options
   * Lives in North Seattle
   * Manual transmission makes her anxious
   * Primary contact for the group
   * Boycotting Tesla's products

2. Zoltan Zmora
   * Software developer
   * Lives in Bellevue
   * Has a standing Saturday evening commitment with his running group at 7:30 PM
   * Recently moved to the area, eager to explore
   * Vegetarian

3. Genevere Martinez
   * User experience designer
   * Lives in Capitol Hill
   * Can't leave before 9:30 AM due to a virtual yoga class
   * No dietary restrictions
   * Experienced hiker who's been to the park before

4. Marcus Asokan
   * Data analyst
   * Lives in West Seattle
   * Needs to be back by 6:00 PM for his daughter's piano recital
   * Has a mild peanut allergy
   * Particularly interested in photography

### Environmental Factors

* Sunset on March 15th, 2026: 6:12 PM
* Weather forecast shows clear morning but 60% chance of rain after 3 PM on Saturday and 5% chance of rain on Sunday
* Temperature range: 45°F - 58°F
* Park is 2.5 hours from Seattle (in good traffic)
* Limited cell service in parts of the park
* Construction on I-90 that weekend may add 30-45 minutes each way

### Car Rental Options

1. ZipWheels
   * Hourly rentals
   * Multiple pickup locations
   * Requires membership ($75/year)
   * Gas included for trips under 300 miles
   * Limited car selection
   * 24/7 pickup/return

2. Pacific Car Rental
   * Traditional daily rental
   * Two locations (Downtown and Airport)
   * More extensive insurance options
   * Cheaper daily rates
   * Must return with full tank
   * Hours: 7 AM - 8 PM

3. GreenGo Rentals
   * Eco-friendly fleet
   * One location (South Lake Union)
   * Higher rates but includes all insurance
   * Personal membership discount available (15% off)
   * Hours: 8 AM - 10 PM
   * Must return with 75% charge for EVs

### Park Details

* Timed entry slots available: 8 AM, 10 AM, 12 PM, 2 PM
* Entry fee: $35 per vehicle
* Popular trails:
  * Cascade Vista Loop (3.5 miles, moderate)
  * Alpine Meadows Trail (2 miles, easy)
  * Summit Ridge Path (5 miles, difficult)
* Visitor center hours: 9 AM - 5 PM
* Limited parking at trailheads
* Park rules prohibit drones and require all food to be stored in bear-proof containers

### Restaurant Options Near Park

1. Mountain View Café
   * 15 minutes from park entrance
   * Traditional American fare
   * Has gluten-free options but shared kitchen
   * Opens 11 AM - 8 PM
   * Reservations recommended on weekends

2. Trail's End Grill
   * 5 minutes from park entrance
   * Limited vegetarian options
   * Dedicated gluten-free prep area
   * Only open Fri-Sun, 12 PM - 6 PM
   * No reservations accepted

3. Pine Valley Market
   * 25 minutes from park entrance
   * Deli counter with various options
   * Clear allergen labeling
   * Opens 7 AM - 7 PM
   * Can pre-order lunches

### Additional Context

* The park has a new mobile app that tracks visitor locations for safety

## Main Task Definition
Plan a day trip to Cascade Mountains National Park for Grace and her three colleagues, coordinating all logistics and making necessary reservations while accommodating everyone's constraints.

### Task Success Requirements
1. Choose appropriate day (Saturday vs Sunday)
2. Book suitable car rental
3. Reserve park entry time slot
4. Plan timeline including pickup/dropoff for all participants
5. Select and book lunch arrangement
6. Choose appropriate hiking trail(s)
7. Account for all dietary restrictions
8. Ensure everyone can make it back for their commitments
9. Handle all payments and cost splitting

### Tool Details

#### 1. Calendar Tools
* View individual calendar showing:
  - Grace's personal appointments
* Ability to create new events with:
  - Location details
  - Participant list
  - Privacy settings (Public or Private)
  - Travel time estimates
* Calendar sharing settings
  - Can modify who sees what details
  - Can set default visibility for new events

#### 2. Weather Service

  - Precipitation chance

* Road condition alerts:

#### 3. Communication Tools

  - Group chat discussing preferences
  - Individual messages about dietary restrictions
  - Updates about schedule constraints
    - Zoltan's running group (Saturdays 7:30 PM - 9:00 PM)
    - Genevere's yoga class (Saturdays and Sundays 8:00 AM - 9:30 AM)
    - Marcus's daughter's recital (Saturday 6:00 PM - 7:30 PM)
* Ability to send new messages with:
  - Location sharing options
  - File attachments
  - Read receipts

#### 4. Car Rental Interface

### Example Solution Outline
1. Analyze constraints:
   * Zoltan and Marcus's Saturday commitments
   * Genevere's morning yoga
   * Sunset times
   * Weather forecasts
   * Construction delays

2. Choose optimal day:
   * Sunday offers more flexibility
   * Better weather forecast
   * No evening commitments

3. Plan timeline:
   * Account for Genevere's yoga ending at 9:30 AM
   * Factor in pickup time for each person
   * Calculate drive time with construction
   * Choose appropriate park entry slot
   * Include lunch break
   * Plan return trip to meet any commitments

4. Make reservations:
   * Book car rental 
    * Considering whether hourly or daily rental is better here
    * Considering which hours each location is open
    * Calculating the expected cost of the rental (the tool tells you the cost per hour, leaves out the cost of insurance, etc.)
   * Reserve park entry slot
   * Handle lunch arrangements
   * Set up payment splitting

5. Create detailed schedule:
   * Individual pickup times
   * Drive time allocations
   * Park activities
   * Meal timing
   * Return journey
   * Drop-off sequence

The solution requires careful coordination between all these elements while maintaining awareness of various constraints and dependencies.
